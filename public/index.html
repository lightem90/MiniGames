MeetingBreak

Paste your meeting invite (or just describe it), add your colleagues' faces, get a 60-second game about it.

Copy it from Teams, Outlook or Google Calendar, or just describe the meeting in a few words.
Where do I find my invite?

Teams / Outlook: open the meeting, click in the details, Ctrl+A, Ctrl+C, paste here. Classic Outlook can also do File → Save As → .ics.

Google Calendar / Meet: open the event, select the title, time and guest list, copy, paste here. The invite e-mail in Gmail also has an invite.ics attachment.

Names and duration are all the game needs. E-mail addresses are removed before anything leaves this browser.

📸 Put your colleagues in the game Win+Shift+S a face from the call, then Ctrl+V here or drop photos here / click to pick, up to 5. They never leave this browser.

Optional: sign in so you are never one of the targets.

Privacy: your images never leave this browser and are not put in share links. Only the text you type (and your first name, if you sign in) is sent to build the game, with email addresses removed. Nothing is stored. You are responsible for the images you use.
